FreeRope Privacy Policy

Last updated October 30, 2026

FreeRope is a jump counter for Crossrope AMP handles. It works fully on your device. An account is optional and only needed for the global leaderboard.

What FreeRope stores

Your workouts (jump counts, times, pace and calories) and the profile you enter (height, weight, age, sex and rope weight) are stored only on your phone or in your browser. FreeRope uses your profile only to estimate calories.

Without an account

FreeRope collects nothing. Your workouts and profile stay on your device and aren't sent to us or to anyone else.

With an account (optional)

If you sign in to use the leaderboard, FreeRope stores:

  • Your sign-in: the email address from Apple, Google or the email you enter, so you can sign in again. If you use Sign in with Apple and hide your email, we only get Apple's private relay address.
  • Your leaderboard name. It's public on the leaderboard.
  • A summary of each workout: date, type, duration, jumps, pace, best streak, best minute, calories and rope weight. Only your totals and bests are shown on the leaderboard; the individual workouts are private to you.

Your height, weight, age and sex never leave your device. FreeRope has no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it never sells or shares your data. Accounts and leaderboards run on Supabase, which stores this data for us.

Anyone can report a leaderboard name that's offensive or impersonates someone; reported names may be hidden.

Bluetooth

FreeRope uses Bluetooth only to connect to your Crossrope handles and read your jump count. It doesn't use Bluetooth to locate you.

Network

The Android app checks ramonmm3.github.io/freerope for new versions. That request contains no personal data.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app (or clearing the site's data in your browser) deletes everything FreeRope stored on your device. To delete your account, open Profile → Delete Account in the app: your name and every workout summary are removed from our servers right away.
