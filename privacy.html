FreeRope Privacy Policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

FreeRope is a jump counter for Crossrope AMP handles. It's built so that your data never leaves your device.

What FreeRope stores

Your workouts (jump counts, times, pace and calories) and the profile you enter (height, weight, age, sex and rope weight) are stored only on your phone or in your browser. FreeRope uses your profile only to estimate calories.

What FreeRope collects

Nothing. FreeRope has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it doesn't send your workouts or profile to us or to anyone else.

Bluetooth

FreeRope uses Bluetooth only to connect to your Crossrope handles and read your jump count. It doesn't use Bluetooth to locate you.

Network

The Android app checks ramonmm3.github.io/freerope for new versions. That request contains no personal data.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app (or clearing the site's data in your browser) deletes everything FreeRope stored.

Contact

Questions: ramonmm3@gmail.com

FreeRope is a fan project and isn't affiliated with Crossrope.
